# Episode 02: Letters from No One

August arrived with a heat that seemed to press the Dursleys' house flat. Harry spent his days feeding the garden, clearing the fireplace, and wondering why no one at school had written to him. Then, on a perfectly ordinary Tuesday, the first letter came.

It was addressed in emerald-green ink:

**Mr H. Potter**<br>
**The Cupboard under the Stairs**<br>
**Privet Drive**<br>
**Little Whinging**<br>
**Surrey**

Before Harry could open the envelope, Vernon Dursley snatched it away. He turned it over, saw the heavy red seal, and ordered Harry from the room. The next day, the postman brought another letter. This time it was addressed to the smallest bedroom.

By the end of the week, letters were everywhere. They came through the mail slot and fluttered behind the kitchen door. They arrived on breakfast trays and rattled down the chimney. Vernon burned one, then two, until the living room smelled of scorched parchment.

"I want to read it!" Harry shouted.

"No," Vernon snapped. "No magic in this house!"

As more envelopes appeared, Petunia grew pale. Dudley, for once, stopped eating. Vernon dragged the family on a frantic journey away from Privet Drive. They slept in a damp hotel, then a lonely cottage on a rock in the middle of the sea.

That night, the letters found them too.

At midnight, something hammered at the door. The island shook. Vernon seized a rifle and stood in the corridor while Harry pressed himself against the wall.

The door burst from its hinges.

On the threshold stood a towering man in a tattered coat, with a tangled beard and eyes as bright as coals.

"Couldn't make us a cup o' tea, could yeh?" he said. "It's not been an easy journey."

He filled the doorway as the Dursleys tried to hide behind their table. The giant looked down at Harry and grinned.

"You're Harry Potter," he said.

From Harry's expression, he knew the answer.
